Prepare the service

A place for every part of Sunday.

Keep the songs you sing, the slides you reuse, and the order of service in one place. When Sunday comes, your volunteers have a clear view of what’s on screen and what’s next.

A library that grows with you

Find songs by title, lyric or CCLI number. Group them into lists for Christmas, Communion or any occasion, and see when you last sang them.

Put the service in order

Drag songs, readings and slides into a service playlist. Start from a reusable template and adjust the verse and chorus order for this week.

Give every slide a purpose

Build Scripture, announcements, giving and contact slides. Design your own slides, save them for next time, and add images, video or a countdown.

Ready for the projector

Give the room its own display while the operator sees the current slide and what’s next. Use a clicker or keyboard shortcuts and style the output with your church’s colours and logo.

From song library to projector.

Watch a short demo of building a service playlist and running it with the mouse.

Read the walkthrough
  1. Search the song library for a hymn, then click the + beside it to add it to the service playlist.
  2. Click Scripture below the playlist, enter the reading, then click Add to playlist.
  3. Click Present to open a separate projector window.
  4. Click Next or Back to move through the slides, or click a thumbnail to jump directly to it.

Phone remote

Move the slides from anywhere in the room.

Hand the worship leader the controls or run the service from the sound desk. Scan the QR code, enter your PIN, and use the remote in your phone’s browser on the same Wi-Fi.

  • See the current slide and what’s next
  • Go to the next slide, skip songs or jump to a service item
  • Black out the screen, show your church logo or freeze the output

Export and share

Send the service ahead.

Export your playlist as a PDF with one slide per page. Share it with your team for rehearsal, or save a PowerPoint copy with the slide text in the speaker notes.

Moving to another computer? Back up your library or share songs, playlists, themes and saved slides with a Worship Free pack.
